# Bringing the World to Life

One of the first challenges in developing Guanche Warriors
was creating the foundations for exploration.

## Character Movement

The player character needs to move naturally through the
environment while maintaining precise control.

## NPC Interactions

Exploration is not only about moving through the world.

Interacting with NPCs is essential to make the environment
feel alive and give players a reason to explore.

## What's Next?

Continue expanding the interaction system and build the
foundations for a richer exploration experience.
