# Bringing the World to Life

One of the first challenges in developing Guanche Warriors was creating the foundations for exploration.

Before building more complex gameplay systems, I needed to establish how the player moves through the environment and interacts with the world.

## Character Movement

Character movement is one of the first systems players experience in a game.

The goal is to make navigation feel natural, responsive and consistent.

Working on this system has been an opportunity to practise fundamental programming concepts while learning how Unity handles player input and movement.

## NPC Interactions

Exploration is not only about moving through the environment.

Interacting with NPCs is an important part of making a game world feel alive.

The initial interaction system allows the player to communicate with characters through dialogue, establishing a foundation for future development.

## What I Learned

Building these systems has helped me understand the importance of separating responsibilities and creating code that can be expanded as the project grows.

Even seemingly simple features require planning, testing and iteration.

## What's Next?

The next step is to continue refining exploration and interaction systems, focusing on improving the overall player experience.

Each improvement brings Guanche Warriors a little closer to its first playable demo.
