## Starting on paper

One of the ideas I've been working on for Guanche Warriors is a basic puzzle that uses the *physical* and *spiritual* worlds (I will elaborate on these two concepts on a later post). I had a rough concept in mind, so I grabbed some markers and drew a simple layout on paper to figure out how the different paths would connect and how the player could move between them. I won't explain the actual mechanic just yet, but the drawing helped me organise the idea before jumping into Unity.

## Bringing it into Unity

With the sketch as a reference, I started building the same layout in Unity using basic shapes, walls and different colours to distinguish the two worlds. I wasn't worried about graphics at this point; I just wanted a space where I could test the idea. As you can see in the cover, the result is pretty rough, but it's enough for what I need right now.

Moving from paper to Unity also brought up a few things I hadn't considered, especially the scale of the environment, the distances between different areas and how the camera would show the puzzle. What looked straightforward in the drawing needed some adjustments once I could actually move around the scene.

## Next steps

For now, I have the basic layout of the puzzle, but the mechanic itself still needs to be implemented. The next step is to work on the interactions between the physical and spiritual worlds and see whether the idea works as intended.

I'm sure I'll have to change a few things along the way, but that's why I wanted to build this prototype before spending time on proper assets or visuals. At least now I have something to work with instead of just a drawing.
