# The Beginning of Guanche Warriors

Every game starts with an idea. Guanche Warriors started with a
simple ambition: create an original JRPG inspired by the Canary Islands.

## The Vision

Guanche Warriors is an independent 2.5D turn-based RPG inspired by
the landscapes, culture and heritage of the Canary Islands.

The adventure revolves around eight warriors representing the
eight islands, exploring a world where the physical and spiritual
dimensions are closely connected.

Our goal is to combine classic JRPG mechanics with an original
setting, memorable characters and a distinctive visual identity.

## Building the Game

This project is also a journey into game development.

I'm building Guanche Warriors with Unity, learning and implementing
different systems along the way:

- Character movement and exploration.
- NPC interaction and dialogue.
- Turn-based combat mechanics.
- Character progression and transformations.
- Physical and spiritual worlds.

Each feature represents a new technical challenge and an opportunity
to improve as a game developer.

## Why a Devlog?

I want to document this journey from the early prototypes to a
playable demo.

This devlog will cover programming challenges, technical decisions,
game design, visual development and lessons learned throughout
production.

Not just the finished results, but also the process behind them.

## What's Next?

The next step is to continue building the foundations of the game,
refining its core systems and gradually bringing its world to life.

Welcome to the development journey of Guanche Warriors.
